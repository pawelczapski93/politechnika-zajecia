# Basic Mechanical Engineering: interactive lectures

Interactive lecture pages for **Basic Mechanical Engineering** (W01-MEC-1SO-00667-00, Lodz University of Technology, Mechanical Engineering in English, semester 2). The course has 20 h of lectures, 40 h of tutorials, 25 h of laboratory and 6 ECTS.

Open **`index.html`** in a browser. It contains the teaching proposal (lecture format, semester schedule, lab and tutorial alignment, assessment) and links to the ten lectures. The pages work offline and need no installation or internet.

| # | File | Topic |
|---|------|-------|
| 1 | `lecture01.html` | Statics of a particle and of a rigid body |
| 2 | `lecture02.html` | Distributed forces, centroids, trusses, friction |
| 3 | `lecture03.html` | Kinematics of a particle |
| 4 | `lecture04.html` | Plane motion of a rigid body, ICR |
| 5 | `lecture05.html` | Newton's laws, momentum, angular momentum |
| 6 | `lecture06.html` | Systems of particles, moment of inertia, kinetic energy |
| 7 | `lecture07.html` | Axial loading, tensile test, statically indeterminate bars |
| 8 | `lecture08.html` | Shear, joints, Mohr's circle, 3D Hooke's law |
| 9 | `lecture09.html` | Bending: V/M diagrams, moments of inertia, bending stress |
| 10 | `lecture10.html` | Torsion and power transmission, exam briefing |

Keyboard: `→`/`←` next/previous section, `T` lecturer notes (hidden by default), `P` larger text for the projector, `D` dark mode.

Shared code lives in `assets/` (`lecture.css`, `lecture.js`). The list of lectures is defined once, in `BME.LECTURES` in `assets/lecture.js`.
